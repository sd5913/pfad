One number.
One response.

A no-install creative-tool rehearsal. You are the sender: enter a number and press Send value. The browser is the receiver: it turns that number into the opacity of an orange shape. This is one page, not OSC or two connected programs.

  1. Sender: number
  2. Boundary: Send value
  3. Receiver: canvas opacity
  4. Stop: reset to 0%

Try 0.25, 0.75, 3 (clamped to 1), or clear the field (missing input). After 3 seconds without a new send, the artwork resets.

Opacity: 0%Stopped: safe blank state.

Draw the four steps on paper. Predict what 0.75 shows, then send it. Change the mapping in control.js (for example, reverse it) and compare. Press Stop, clear the input, or wait three seconds: does the visual return to the safe state? The text readout gives the same result without relying on colour.
